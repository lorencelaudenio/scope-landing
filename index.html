Why Scope

A recorder that gets out of your way

Capture

Pick which screen to record

Running dual monitors? Choose exactly which display to capture before you start — no mixing up screens.

Storage

1280×720, H.264

Encoded to keep files small without looking compressed — recordings don't quietly eat your disk.

Output

Auto-saved to your Desktop

Every recording lands as an MP4 on your Desktop the moment you stop — no export step, no save dialog.

Focus

Mouse-follow zoom

The recording smoothly zooms and pans to follow your cursor, so viewers' eyes go where yours do.

Camera

Floating camera that zooms on hover

Your webcam bubble sits over the screen and zooms in when you hover it — press Esc to snap back to normal.

Audio

Choose your mic

Record with your Mac's built-in mic or switch to any external one you've got plugged in.
