# Privacy Policy for Scope

**Last updated: [DATE]**

Scope ("the app," "we," "our") is a screen recording application for macOS. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

## 1. Data Collection

Scope does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or any third party. Scope does not include analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.

## 2. Permissions Used

To function, Scope requests the following macOS system permissions:

- **Screen Recording** — to capture your screen content.
- **Camera** — to optionally record webcam video alongside your screen.
- **Microphone** — to optionally record audio narration.

These permissions are used solely to perform the recording features you initiate within the app. Recordings are captured locally on your Mac.

## 3. Data Storage

All recordings, screenshots, and any associated files created by Scope are stored **locally on your device**, in a location you choose or the app's default output folder. Scope does not upload recordings to any server operated by us.

## 4. Data Sharing

Because Scope does not collect or transmit data, we do not share any data with third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Scope does not integrate third-party SDKs, cloud storage services, or external APIs. 

## 6. Your Control Over Data

Since all recordings remain on your device, you retain full control over them — including deleting, moving, or sharing them at your discretion. You can revoke Screen Recording, Camera, or Microphone permissions at any time via **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Scope is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, as it collects no data at all.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
